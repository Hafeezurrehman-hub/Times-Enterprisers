FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How can I find a home tutor?

Call 0345-7888017 or 0340-3788847, message us on WhatsApp, or fill in the request form on this page with the class, subject and your preferred tutor type. We will get back to you to arrange a suitable tutor.

Do you provide online tutors?

Yes, online tutors are one of our services. Choose “Online Tutor” as your preferred tutor type in the form, or tell us when you call.

Are male and female tutors available?

Yes. Both male and female tutors are available. Tell us which you prefer when you contact us.

Which academic levels do you cover?

We offer classes for juniors and seniors, Matric, F.Sc., and O and A Levels, including Cambridge, Edexcel and Oxford curricula, as well as language and entry test preparation.

Do you provide O Level and A Level tutors?

Yes, we offer classes for O Levels and A Levels, including Cambridge, Edexcel and Oxford curricula. Contact us with your subject and we will help you from there.

Can I request a tutor for a specific subject?

Yes. You can choose a subject in the request form. Subjects shown on this page include Science, Biology, Computer Science and Arts. If you need another subject, call or WhatsApp us and ask.

Do you provide Quran tutors?

Yes, Quran teaching is one of our services. Contact us for details and to arrange a tutor.

How can I contact Times Enterprises?

Call 0345-7888017 or 0340-3788847, message us on WhatsApp at 0345-7888017, use the request form on this page, or reach us through our social media pages linked in the footer.
